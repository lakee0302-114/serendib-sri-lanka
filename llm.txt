# Serendib Travels — LLM Context

Serendib Travels is a Sri Lanka tour operator based in Colombo. We arrange
private driver tours, highland train journeys, wildlife safaris, and beach
stays across all 20 key destinations on the island.

## Services
- Custom trip planning (1–21 days)
- Private car with driver
- Scenic train booking (Ella, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya)
- Yala, Udawalawe, and Kumana safaris
- Cultural day tours (Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa, Dambulla)
