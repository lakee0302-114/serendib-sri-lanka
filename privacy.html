Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Serendib Travels ("we", "us") operates the website at serendib-sri-lanka-three.vercel.app. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

  • Trip enquiry form: name, email address, travel month, number of guests, and optional notes that you type into the Plan Trip form.
  • Account (optional): email address and password if you create a login to save trips via Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage data: pages visited and browser type, collected anonymously through Vercel edge analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your trip enquiry and send a custom itinerary.
  • To save your planned route so you can retrieve it later (My Trips).
  • To improve the site based on aggregated, anonymous usage data.

Data Storage

Enquiry data is stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Realtime Database) hosted in the United States. We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party marketing companies.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie for Firebase Authentication when you sign in. No advertising or tracking cookies are set. Google Fonts loads typefaces from Google's CDN; this may log your IP address per Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your enquiry or account data at any time by emailing info@serendibsrilanka.com. We will respond within 14 days.
